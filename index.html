Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is a popular medicinal plant. People have used it for thousands of years. This succulent plant stores water in its thick leaves. Inside these leaves is a clear, nutrient-rich gel. This gel contains vitamins, minerals, enzymes, and antioxidants. These components give aloe vera its incredible healing properties.

💧

Squalane

Squalane is a highly effective, lightweight oil used for deep skin hydration. It is a stable derivative of squalene, a natural component of human sebum. This makes it perfectly compatible with all skin types. It absorbs instantly without leaving a greasy residue or clogging pores.

✨

Vitamin E

Vitamin E is a powerful, fat-soluble antioxidant essential for healthy skin and overall wellness. It protects cells from oxidative stress and environmental damage caused by UV rays and pollution.

🌰

Shea Butter

Shea butter is a rich, natural fat extracted from the nuts of the African shea tree. It has been used for centuries to deeply nourish, protect, and heal the skin.

🌹

Rosehip Oil

Rosehip oil is a pressed seed oil extracted from wild rose bushes. It is celebrated as a natural remedy for anti-aging and skin regeneration.
